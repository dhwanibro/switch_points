Moral Switch Points

Participant Information

This study looks at how people make decisions in moral scenarios. You will read a short scenario and repeatedly choose between two options. It takes about 5–10 minutes.

The scenarios involve hypothetical life-and-death decisions, so some people may feel mild discomfort. Participation is voluntary. We do not collect your name, email or any other directly identifying information. We record your Prolific ID only to process payment and to match your responses to your submission; it is stored separately from your choices and is never published.

You may stop at any point by closing this page or returning the study on Prolific. If you want your data removed after finishing, message the researcher through Prolific within 14 days; we can find and delete it using your Prolific ID. After that, Prolific IDs are removed and the remaining data is fully anonymous and can no longer be withdrawn. Anonymised data (without Prolific IDs) may be shared on open-science platforms.

Instructions

You will go through several short parts. In every part you see the same kind of scenario with two options, but the chances change from part to part. Choose the option you would actually prefer. There are no right or wrong answers. You can click an option or press the ← / → arrow key shown on it.

After each choice you will see the outcome, then press Continue (or the Enter key).

The situation is the same, but the chances for the braking system have changed. Read the options carefully.
